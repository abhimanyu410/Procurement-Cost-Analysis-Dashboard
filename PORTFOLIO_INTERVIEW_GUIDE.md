# Portfolio Interview Guide

## Project 1 — Procurement Cost & Supplier Performance Dashboard
**Problem:** Purchasing teams need a quick view of spend, supplier concentration, lead time, and delivery reliability.
**Approach:** Load CSV data with Pandas, filter supplier/category, calculate KPIs, and visualize with Plotly.
**Be transparent:** The dataset is synthetic, not real company savings or supplier performance.

## Project 2 — Inventory Demand Forecasting
**Problem:** Historical demand can provide a basic reference for inventory planning.
**Approach:** Visualize monthly demand and compare actual demand with a trailing three-month moving-average baseline.
**Limitations:** This is not a validated production forecast and does not optimize reorder points.

## Project 3 — Purchase Assistant Search Prototype
**Problem:** Buyers need to find products and compare indicative catalogue details.
**Approach:** Keyword matching, category filtering, and purchase-order lookup against synthetic CSV files.
**Limitations:** Rule-based search, not a generative AI model; no live-system integration.

## Interview structure
1. State the business question.
2. Explain the dataset and loading/cleaning.
3. Walk through calculations and filters.
4. Demonstrate the dashboard or search.
5. Explain limitations and future improvements.
